QuantForge is a small compiler, built on MLIR, that generates GPU kernels for **low-precision LLM inference**: INT4, INT8 and FP8 weights multiplied with fp16 activations. These notes are the engineering log of the project: how the kernels are generated, which ideas gave speed-ups, which ones did not, and every number with the method that produced it.

The headline results on a laptop RTX 4050 are modest and honest: at batch 1 the generated kernels match or beat [Marlin](https://github.com/IST-DASLab/marlin), the hand-written reference, and a 1B-parameter model decodes about 2.2x faster than in fp16. At batch sizes 32 to 256 the kernels are 12 to 33 percent slower than Marlin, and one post explains why with data.
