QuantForge Notes
PostsAboutGitHubTiếng Việt

QuantForge is a small compiler, built on MLIR, that generates GPU kernels for low-precision LLM inference: INT4, INT8 and FP8 weights multiplied with fp16 activations. These notes are the engineering log of the project: how the kernels are generated, which ideas gave speed-ups, which ones did not, and every number with the method that produced it.

The headline results on a laptop RTX 4050 are modest and honest: at batch 1 the generated kernels are on par with Marlin, the hand-written reference, for 4096 x 4096 layers (85 to 97 percent on wider ones), and a 1B-parameter model decodes about 2.2x faster than in fp16. At batch sizes 32 to 256 the kernels are 13 to 33 percent slower than Marlin, and one post explains why with data. One post is also an erratum: an earlier, too-good-to-be-true figure did not survive re-measurement.
